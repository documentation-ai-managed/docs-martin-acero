## Overview

Easily manage your documentation by creating dedicated projects.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Projects">
    ````
    Navigate to the "Projects" section from the main menu.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Add Project'">
    ````
    Look for the "Add Project" button and click it.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill details and save">
    ````
    Enter your project name and description, then click "Save" to create the project.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
