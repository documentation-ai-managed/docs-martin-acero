---
title: Document Not Saving
description: What to do if your documentation isn't saving in martin acero.
---

## Symptoms

- Changes aren't reflected after saving
- Error: "Failed to save document"

## Causes

- Browser offline or unstable
- Session expired

## Solution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your connection">
    ````
    Ensure your device is online and try refreshing the page.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in again">
    ````
    If prompted, sign out and log back in to martin acero.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
