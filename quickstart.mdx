## Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have:

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Safari)
- An email address for account verification
- Internet connection

<Callout kind="info">

Martin Acero is a cloud-based platform. No local installation required.

</Callout>

## Create Your Account

Sign up in under 2 minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Dashboard" icon="monitor">
    Go to `https://dashboard.martin-acero.com` and click **Sign Up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Details" icon="user">
    Provide your email, name, and a strong password. Accept the terms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email" icon="mail">
    Check your inbox for the verification link and click it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log In" icon="log-in">
    Return to the dashboard, enter your credentials, and access your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create Your First Documentation Project

Once logged in, create a new space for your project docs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="New Project" icon="plus">
    Click **New Space** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Space" icon="settings">
    Enter a name like `My Project Docs`, select visibility (`Public` or `Private`), and choose a slug (e.g., `my-project`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate Initial Pages" icon="file-text">
    Martin Acero auto-creates `introduction.mdx`, `quickstart.mdx`, and `changelog.mdx`. Customize them next.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set Up Initial Content

Edit `quickstart.mdx` to add your first custom content.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Add a Heading" icon="type">
    ```

    Start writing your docs here.

    ```

  <Tab title="Insert Code Example" icon="code">

    ```javascript
